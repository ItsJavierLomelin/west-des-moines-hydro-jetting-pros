Hydro jetting or snaking?

Mechanical rodding pushes a bladed rod through a blockage. Jetting directs pressurized water at the debris. Which one fits depends on the obstruction and the pipe.

Questions

Questions worth asking before hydro jetting

Is hydro jetting suitable for every pipe?

No. Ask a qualified professional to assess the line, access and blockage before recommending high-pressure cleaning.

Can cleaning repair a broken sewer pipe?

Cleaning removes an obstruction. It does not rebuild a damaged pipe. Ask whether an inspection shows a repair issue as well as a blockage.

What details should I give with a request?

List the affected fixtures, whether wastewater has backed up and any earlier cleaning. Mention known pipe repairs or access limits.

Is service available at my address?

Availability has to be confirmed for the address and the work. A request is not a booked appointment or a promise of service.

Who treats wastewater from West Des Moines?

West Des Moines is listed as a member city of the Des Moines Metropolitan Wastewater Reclamation Authority, which conveys and treats wastewater for the metro. A backup inside a house still starts with the house line.

Do older and newer parts of the city need different questions?

Often. Valley Junction and the annexed communities have older lines, while the west side was built in the 1990s and 2000s. Ask for an inspection of the actual pipe.
